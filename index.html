    What it takes to make it a Fleet Buy

    Offer

    Strong buy: asking is at or under your opening offer and the score is 75+. Moderate buy: asking is at or under your walk-away price and the score is 60+. Weak buy: asking is over walk-away, the score is under 60, the serial isn't verified, or there's no usable paperwork. Bill of sale only caps the rating at Moderate and adds the paperwork allowance from Settings ($300 to start); a title name mismatch or no paperwork adds twice that.

    Worth it when done?

    Net sale = market value minus cost to sell (fees, ads, transport). Break-even = the most you can pay the seller and still get your money back if you sell. To sell, price at or above the minimum sale price shown.
